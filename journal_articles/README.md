# Dedicated Reference Library: Humanisation of Cattle Gut Microbiome & Resistome

This directory contains full-text Open Access research articles and foundational literature directly supporting the study design, hypotheses, and One Health narrative of the **Humanisation of the Cattle Gut Microbiome** research paper.

---

## Downloaded Full-Text Articles

| File | Journal & Year | Title | Core Contribution to Our Paper |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| [`2022_eLife_Dillard_Humanization_of_wildlife_gut_microbiota_in_urban_environments.pdf`](./2022_eLife_Dillard_Humanization_of_wildlife_gut_microbiota_in_urban_environments.pdf) | *eLife* (2022) | **Humanization of wildlife gut microbiota in urban environments** | **Foundational Paradigm:** Demonstrates that urban animals acquire human-associated gut microbes and enteric lineages (e.g. *Bacteroides*). Directly anchors our hypothesis that urban stray cattle experience microbiome "humanisation". |
| [`2019_NatCommun_Liu_Fecal_resistome_cattle_associated_with_diet.pdf`](./2019_NatCommun_Liu_Fecal_resistome_cattle_associated_with_diet.pdf) | *Nature Communications* (2019) | **The fecal resistome of dairy cattle is associated with diet during nursing** | **Diet-Resistome Link:** Proves that dietary shifts dynamically drive the succession and abundance of antimicrobial resistance genes (ARGs) and metal resistance in the bovine gut. |
| [`2026_NatCommun_Gicquel_Farming_practices_selection_pressures_resistome.pdf`](./2026_NatCommun_Gicquel_Farming_practices_selection_pressures_resistome.pdf) | *Nature Communications* (2026) | **Farming practices exert selection pressures on the resistome of natural populations of house mice** | **Anthropogenic Pressure on Mobile ARGs:** Illustrates how human land-use and agricultural intensity select for mobile genetic elements (integrons, *sul1*, *tet*) in animal populations. |
| [`2026_FrontMicrobiol_Guo_Gut_microbiome_AMR_transmission_livestock_OneHealth.pdf`](./2026_FrontMicrobiol_Guo_Gut_microbiome_AMR_transmission_livestock_OneHealth.pdf) | *Frontiers in Microbiology* (2026) | **The role of gut microbiome in antimicrobial resistance transmission between companion animals and livestock: mechanisms, drivers, and One Health implications** | **One Health Framework:** Provides mechanistic pathways of horizontal gene transfer (conjugation, integrons) and cross-species AMR transmission between livestock, environments, and human populations. |
| [`2026_FrontPubHealth_Vivarelli_Resistome_bridge_livestock_workers_AMR.pdf`](./2026_FrontPubHealth_Vivarelli_Resistome_bridge_livestock_workers_AMR.pdf) | *Frontiers in Public Health* (2026) | **The resistome bridge between livestock and workers: novel frameworks for early detection and monitoring of antimicrobial resistance** | **Resistome Spillover Bridge:** Describes metagenomic monitoring of ARG transmission at animal-human interfaces and supports our recommendation of stray cattle as environmental AMR sentinels. |
