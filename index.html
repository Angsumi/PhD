Humanisation of the Cattle Gut Microbiome

What happens to a cattle microbiome in a human world? A comparative metagenomics study of domestic vs. urban stray cattle across Assam to uncover humanisation signatures, dietary impacts, antimicrobial resistance, and One Health implications.

2 Districts Assam field sampling: matched breed & gender across village domestic and urban stray populations for climatic & genetic uniformity

Two ways of living
besides humans.

Two distinct populations living in close proximity to humans provide two answers to the same question: how does human proximity shape the animal gut microbiome?

Domestic cattle

Managed proximity

Maintained under thousands of years of cultural management in structured farming systems with stable routines.

  • Thousand years of cultural management
  • Controlled, structured diet
  • Very low antibiotic exposure
  • Sheltered, managed living environment
Urban stray cattle

Unmanaged proximity

Free-roaming animals thriving in human settlements, navigating street environments and municipal waste streams.

  • Shared streets and public spaces
  • Variable food and environmental exposure
  • Rely on scavenged food and waste
  • High potential antibiotic exposure
Between two groups, there is a signal. A measurable “Humanisation Signature” revealing how human-associated diets, waste, and environments reshape gut microbiota and drive resistome expansion.

Methodological workflow

8-step research pipeline.

From rigorous field design in Assam to high-throughput metagenomics, Gene Ontology annotation, KEGG pathway networks, and One Health impact.

01

Faecal Matter Collection & Field Design

Strategic geographic sampling across two districts in Assam: 2 village samples and 2 urban cattle from district headquarters per district. Village and city proximity ensures relatedness and identical climatic conditions. Exact same breed and gender selected for uniformity to eliminate breed-related microbiota fluctuations. Fresh faecal collection prevents DNA decay and air contamination.

2 Districts in Assam Village vs Urban HQ Matched Breed & Gender Fresh Faecal Sampling
02

Metagenomic Sequencing

High-throughput shotgun metagenomics sequencing to capture total community DNA. Comprehensive data quality verification upon receipt—accepting datasets that pass stringent depth and error thresholds, or initiating reruns for suboptimal libraries.

Shotgun Metagenomics Quality Verification Strict Rerun Protocol
03

Bioinformatics Pipeline

End-to-end metagenomic processing: raw sequencing data merged with comprehensive metadata, rigorous quality control (QC), adapter/tag removal, taxonomic classification down to order and genus level using QIIME 2, and publication-ready Python visualisations.

Raw & Metadata QC Tag Removal QIIME 2 (Order & Genus) Python Visualisation
04

Functional Annotation (GO Analysis)

Categorise identified microbial genes into clear functional groups using Gene Ontology (GO) analysis. Align assembled sequences against standard internet-accessible Gene Ontology databases to map biological processes, molecular functions, and cellular components.

Gene Ontology (GO) Functional Grouping Database Alignment
05

Pathway & Network Analysis (KEGG)

Kyoto Encyclopedia of Genes and Genomes (KEGG) analysis to uncover the exact metabolic pathways that specific microbiota groups participate in. Perform network analysis mapping interactions between functional groups and discovered pathways to reveal metabolic rewiring.

KEGG Metabolic Pathways Microbial Functional Mapping Pathway Network Analysis
06

Statistical Data Analysis & Biological Interpretation

Directly compare domestic vs. urban stray populations using multivariate statistical tests. Translate omics findings to identify key microbial shifts, evaluate diet-microbiome links, interpret active functions, assess zoonotic risks, examine antimicrobial resistance patterns, and identify predictive biomarkers.

Comparative Statistics Omics Translation Zoonotic Risk AMR Pattern Assessment
07

Research Outcomes

Deliver an integrated body of evidence on Animal, Human, and Environmental interactions. Establish a baseline One Health perspective on stray cattle ecology and provide a definitive assessment of antibiotic resistance gene (ARG) penetration in urban ecosystems.
